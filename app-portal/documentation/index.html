Documentation Philosophy

I believe documentation should:

  • Guide, not overwhelm
  • Respect learners
  • Stay transparent and honest
  • Reflect the emotional side of building software
  • Support collaboration rather than control

"Setbian documentation exists to help people feel included and informed — not lost."
